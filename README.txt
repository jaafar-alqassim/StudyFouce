CyberJ Focus — Upload all files to the root of your GitHub Pages repository. Enable Pages from Settings > Pages. The logo is included inside the app and as the installed app icon. Language and theme preferences are saved in your browser. Notifications while the app is closed are not guaranteed.

Achievements & statistics (v4): From Home, choose Achievements & statistics.
Statistics count saved focus sessions (not unsaved timer time) and currently completed lessons.
Charts show the previous seven days based on the device/browser timezone used by the app (Asia/Bahrain date keys).
Achievements unlock dynamically; they are not cloud-synced. Export a backup before switching devices or browsers.
